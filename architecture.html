Prot****ical · Inbox Watch

How the production system fits together

Your team keeps working in Outlook. Mail rules and a few Outlook buttons do the simple things. A small service in your own Azure keeps a clock on every customer thread, uses AI only where it has to read the email, and reports in Teams and on this dashboard. Nothing is ever sent to customers automatically.

Rules handle anything that can be decided from the sender, recipients, time or a click. AI is used only where the system has to understand the text of an email. Every AI decision can be corrected with a button, and those corrections are kept to improve the filter.

Three key flows

A customer email arrives

  1. Mail lands in orders@; your existing rules file it as today.
  2. Graph tells the service within about a minute.
  3. Noise filter: a system sender or Cc-only copy stops here.
  4. AI classifier: "order, gloves, normal urgency" → 24 h deadline.
  5. Thread history records the start of the clock.

Someone takes it

  1. A team member clicks Handled in Outlook (or Taken in Teams).
  2. The API records who and when, and sets the Outlook category.
  3. The clock stops; the thread leaves every queue and alert.
  4. Waiting on customer pauses instead; the customer's next email restarts the clock.

Nobody takes it

  1. At 75 % of the deadline the scheduler posts a warning card in Teams.
  2. At the deadline: escalation to the person you name.
  3. Still open later: repeat escalation to a manager.
  4. The breach is counted in the dashboard and the Monday digest.

Connections, access and data

Microsoft 365

  • Graph application permission Mail.ReadWrite, limited to the six shared mailboxes by an Exchange access policy. Write is only used for categories.
  • Graph change notifications, renewed automatically; 15-minute sync as a safety net.
  • Teams: one channel, cards posted by the service.
  • Outlook add-in deployed centrally; it signs the user in with their Microsoft account.

Azure

  • All components in your subscription and region.
  • Certificate in Key Vault instead of a password.
  • Dashboard and API behind Microsoft sign-in, Pr**** accounts only.
  • Monitoring alert if the service stops receiving mail.

AI and data

  • Azure OpenAI in your Azure; email text is not used to train models.
  • Only mail that passed the noise filter is sent to the model.
  • Personal mailboxes are never read.
  • Nothing is sent to customers automatically; drafts always go to a person.
